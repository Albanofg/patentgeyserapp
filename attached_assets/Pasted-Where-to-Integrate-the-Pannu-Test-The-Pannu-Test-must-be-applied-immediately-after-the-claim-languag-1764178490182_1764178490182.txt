Where to Integrate the Pannu Test
The Pannu Test must be applied immediately after the claim language is finalized and before the final Provisional Document is compiled.

Stage of Insertion: Stage 4b (Claims Drafting).

Exact Location: Insert the new sequence of nodes after the LLM Agent generates a single claim set (following the current AI Agent and Parse nodes) and before that claim set is merged and sent to the Aggregate All Claims node.

Rationale: This ensures that every individual claim set (Independent Claim + dependents) is legally validated as having a certified human inventor before it becomes part of the final patent application.

⚙️ How the Pannu Test Must Work (Two Asynchronous Sequences)
The Pannu Test requires a mandatory pause for human interaction, necessitating two asynchronous sequences managed by two new, dedicated webhook endpoints.

Sequence 1: Ask the Questions (Initial Trigger)
This sequence runs automatically within your existing claims drafting loop.

New Webhook 1 (e.g., /pannu-prompt-claims):

This webhook is triggered by your existing claims flow, receiving the draft claim text, concept ID, and the crucial White Space Strategy.

Pannu Prompt Engine (New LLM Agent):

Input: Drafted Independent Claim and its White Space Strategy.

Role: Pannu Test Question Generator.

Action: Generates exactly 3-5 structured questions specifically targeted at proving the human contribution meets the Conception, Quality, and Exceeding Known Concepts criteria.

Response to UI (Response Node):

The workflow uses a Response Node to send the structured questions back to your running UI/API. The workflow instance then terminates/pauses, waiting for the human inventor to respond.

Sequence 2: Validate the Answers (Continuation Trigger)
This sequence is initiated by your UI/API layer once the human inventor submits their answers.

New Webhook 2 (e.g., /pannu-validate-answers):

This is the second, dedicated webhook that receives the human inventor's structured answers (the legal testimony) and the context (Claim ID, Claim Text).

Inventorship Validator (New LLM Agent):

Input: The original claim text and the structured human answers.

Role: Inventorship Compliance Scorer.

Action: Compares the human testimony against the technical language of the claim, generating a Pannu Confidence Score (0.0 to 1.0) and a final Certification Status (Certified or Rejected).

Audit and Gate (Conditional Logic):

A Conditional Logic Node checks the Pannu Confidence Score.

TRUE Path: A Database/Log Node creates the Pannu Compliance Record (the audit trail) by logging the claim, answers, and score permanently. The certified claim is then passed to a Merge Node to continue to the Aggregate All Claims node.

FALSE Path: The claim is routed out of the primary flow to be flagged for human attorney review or sent to a claims refinement loop.
