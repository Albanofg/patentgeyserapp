1. A system comprising: a Geo-Contextual Service Registry storing associations between
property location types and external environmental data providers; a location classifier
configured to determine a location type for a property based at least in part on coordinates or
an address; an orchestrator in communication with the Geo-Contextual Service Registry and
configured to select one or more environmental data providers associated with the determined
location type; and an Adaptive Date Intelligence module coupled to the orchestrator and
configured to: receive an indication of a single-night stay request for the property, determine a
property-specific minimum stay requirement, autonomously extend the requested stay to satisfy
the minimum stay requirement based at least in part on real-time market holiday data and
temporal calculations performed in a timezone associated with the property, and output
modified temporal parameters for downstream availability determination.

influence the Adaptive Date Intelligence module’s extension determination.
