Big ideas, distilled
1. Exemplary claims are strategic, not optional

Claims in a provisional are not about allowance.
They are about locking in scope and proving possession.
Well-written exemplary claims materially increase future leverage.

2. Aggressiveness comes from abstraction, not invention

Strong claims:

Generalize functions

Abstract structure

Decouple steps

Avoid implementation details

Aggressiveness never comes from adding undisclosed ideas.

3. One AI task per phase

Avoid bland output and timeouts by splitting work into phases:

Draft

Evaluate

Optimize

Each phase has a narrow objective and a small output.

4. The AI must be legally constrained

Hard rules:

Claims may only rely on disclosed material

Undisclosed ideas are never silently included

Unsupported elements are explicitly flagged

This prevents hallucinated scope.

5. §112(a) support checking is mandatory

Every claim is treated as hostile litigation would treat it.
Unsupported elements are classified by severity.
HIGH severity blocks acceptance.

This is the core safety gate.

6. Claims are scored, not guessed

A fixed rubric turns quality into a measurable target:

Coverage

Abstraction

Design-around resistance

Language breadth

Claim ecosystem strength

The system stops only when the score threshold is met.

7. Iteration beats one-shot brilliance

The engine assumes the first draft is not optimal.
Claims improve through repeated refinement.
Stopping conditions are enforced by the workflow, not the model.

8. Discovery is quarantined, not suppressed

The system may identify powerful new ideas.
Those ideas are:

Clearly labeled as undisclosed

Never added to claims automatically

They become inputs to a separate disclosure expansion workflow.

9. Disclosure expansion is explicit and auditable

If a new idea is approved:

New disclosure text is written

§112(a) is satisfied independently

The updated spec is re-ingested

Claims are regenerated

No silent scope creep.

10. n8n controls logic, AI provides reasoning

The workflow enforces:

Looping

Gates

Stop conditions

Safety checks

The AI never decides when to stop or what rules to ignore.

11. LangChain is used for consolidation, not overload

AI Agents are reused across phases.
Prompts define role, not node type.
Complexity lives in reasoning, not workflow sprawl.

12. Patent Geyser is infrastructure, not a one-off tool

This system is:

Specification-agnostic

Domain-agnostic

Repeatable

Scalable

It is a claims generation platform, not a document generator.

One-sentence vision

Patent Geyser systematically converts disclosed inventions into maximally broad, defensible patent claim ecosystems through controlled, scored, and disclosure-safe AI iteration.
